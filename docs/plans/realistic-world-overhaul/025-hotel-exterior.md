# Plan 025: Shared exterior detail kit, signage and the Grand Hotel facade

Epic E2. Estimated turns: 60.

## Goal

Make buildings look like real South Indian buildings from outside. Build a reusable exterior detail kit (window frames and shutters, door frames, balconies, drainpipes, planters, bins, wall lamps, awnings, porticos, steps, AC units, water tanks, satellite dishes, signboards with Tamil and English text) that all 14 landmarks reuse, a generic `placeExteriorDetails` that builds the manifest `exteriorDetails` for ANY building, then the bespoke Grand Hotel facade (`decoratePlaza`): pastel plaster, portico with columns, balconies, shutters, AC units, a lit signboard, a clay-tile roof over the roof stair, a water tank and a dish on the roof. Tamil text comes from one content file and is a human check (A2).

Decisions fixed here. (1) The seven manifest classes are `window door signage drainpipe planter bin lamp` (ARCH 10.6). Builders of other things (`balcony`, `awning`, `portico`, `steps`, `ac-unit`, `water-tank`, `dish`, `roof-hip`) produce EXTRA decoration: their `detailClass` is `extra` and their `detailKind` names them; extras are never declared in manifests and are counted separately: `countDetails(instance)` returns `{ declaredClasses: { window, door, signage, drainpipe, planter, bin, lamp }, extra: { <detailKind>: n } }`. `doorFrame` has `detailClass` `door`. (2) The decorate hook: `buildBuilding(manifest, ctx, placement, { decorate, skipGeneric })` (plan 021 reserved the fourth argument): `placeExteriorDetails` is called by `buildBuilding` itself after the shell (generic details for every class not listed in `skipGeneric`), then `decorate(instance, ctx)` adds bespoke items. The kit never discovers `build.js` by glob; callers pass it: `src/world/buildings/registry.js` (created here) exports `DECORATORS = { plaza: { decorate: decoratePlaza, skipGeneric: ["signage", "planter", "bin", "lamp"] } }` and `getDecorator(zoneId)` (returns `{}` when none), and the fixtures, `scripts/check-buildings.mjs` and the real world always call `buildBuilding(m, ctx, placement, getDecorator(m.id))`. Export naming `decorate<Id>`. (3) Draw calls: details are merged per material into one group `exterior-details` (at most 14 meshes for the hotel); signboards keep their own material and stay separate; everything this plan adds to the hotel is at most 40 draw calls. The instance keeps `instance.details = [{ detailClass, detailKind, x, y, z }]` (one record per detail object, building-local) which `countDetails` reads. (4) Tier: with `ctx.tier === "low"` (phone) the generic placer builds half (rounded up) of the planter, bin and drainpipe items that are not at an entrance, and the light manager already reduces lamps and sign lights to emissive-only through its pool size; `checkDetails` always builds at tier `high`.
