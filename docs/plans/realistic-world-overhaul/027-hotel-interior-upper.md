Epic E2. Estimated turns: 60.

## Goal

Furnish and light the rest of the Grand Hotel by data: the first floor corridor `corridor-1` with numbered doors, the four guest rooms `guest-1` to `guest-4` (each with a real bed, wardrobe, desk, TV, minibar and its own bathroom `bath-1` to `bath-4`) and the walkable `rooftop`, plus a new rooftop prop set. Then raise the manifest stage to `full`, so the whole Rich bar (`npm run check:buildings`, no flags) passes for the hotel, and prove journey 5 (sit on the bed, first person, stand up) and the roof edge. Plan 024 fixed the structure (rooms, doors, windows, stairs, lift `lift1`), plan 026 furnished the ground floor; this plan changes none of that structure and none of plan 026 entries.

Rules and notation are those of plan 026 (read its goal text and `docs/building-manifest.md`): building-local metres, `y` above the room floor, front of an object at `rot` is `(sin rot, cos rot)` (0 faces +z/south, 3.1416 north, 1.5708 east, -1.5708 west), `type (x, z, rot)` is an object entry, `y 0.6` adds `"y": 0.6` (ceiling-mounted props are modelled for a 3.0 m ceiling; the first floor ceiling is 3.6 m, so y 0.6), `type on:<target> xK` is `place: "on:<target>"` with `count` K, `at:window:<id>` is `place: "window:<id>"` (window ids `w-1` to `w-24` in the order of plan 024), `text` is the optional sign key field. Wall contact: back of the object 0.05 m from the inner wall face (perimeter walls 0.3 m, partitions 0.075 m inside the room bound). Placed lamp props never create lights, the manifest declares each light at the lamp socket position. If `checkBuilding` or the build reports a placement problem for a coordinate below, move or shrink THAT entry by the smallest amount; never weaken a check, never drop a room under its minimum. Geometry facts: floors y 0 / 4.0 / 7.8; `corridor-1` bounds `[-14, -3, 14, 3]` with the stair opening of `stairs-1` (x -13.8 to -6.5, z -1.55 to -0.15) and the lift cabin (x -4 to -2, z -3 to -1, entrance west); the stair `stairs-2` to the roof (x 3.0 to 9.9, z -0.7 to 0.7) and plan 025 hip roof with posts (x 2.6 to 10.3, z -1.3 to 1.3) on the rooftop; guest rooms south `guest-1 [-14, 3, -3, 10]`, `guest-2 [3, 3, 14, 10]`, north `guest-3 [-14, -10, -3, -3]`, `guest-4 [3, -10, 14, -3]`; baths `bath-1 [-3, 3, 0, 10]`, `bath-2 [0, 3, 3, 10]`, `bath-3 [-3, -10, 0, -3]`, `bath-4 [0, -10, 3, -3]`.
