# Plan 029: Town square in front of the hotel: paving, banyan platform, clock tower, street furniture

Epic E2. Estimated turns: 60.

## Goal

Build the public square the visitor sees on leaving the hotel, in a South Indian town style: a paved square with kerbs, a large banyan-style tree on a round stone platform, a clock tower with a working clock face, street lamps that switch on at dusk, benches, bins, planters, direction signposts in English and Tamil and a bus-stop shelter. All positions live in one data file (`src/world/layout/slice.json`) read through `loadSquare()`, so plan 032 can move the square without touching code or frozen tests. Collision, lights and walkability are real.

Decisions fixed here. (1) Coordinates: the hotel is placed at world `(0, -14)`, rotation 0, footprint 28 by 20 (x from -14 to 14, z from -24 to -4), front toward +z; its main door is at hotel-local x -6 so world `(-6, -4)`, the restaurant entrance at `(8, -4)`; the portico of plan 025 protrudes 2.0 m, so the portico front is at z = -2. The square is the rectangle x from -28 to 28, z from -2 to 42 (centre `(0, 20)`, 56 by 44), paving top 0.15 m above the terrain, which is flat at y 0 under the hotel and the square. The platform is at `(0, 20)`. North is -z, so the hotel is at the north edge and the road entrances are west `(-28, 20)`, east `(28, 20)` and south `(0, 42)`. (2) Tests never hard-code these numbers: they read `loadSquare()` (plan 032 and 033 may change its source, never its shape) and negative cases use literal plans written inside the test. (3) The hotel is NOT built into the world by this plan (plan 031 streams it), so the square faces an empty flat plot for now. (4) v1 buildings that overlap the square (zones `plaza`, `notice`, `milestones`, `temple`: found with the helper below) stay in the v1 world until plan 031 moves them, except the v1 `plaza` BUILDING, which this plan stops building (it would stand on the walkway). (5) Real-area objects are reported to the hook through a new array `game.areaObjects` (created in `src/state.js` next to `fixtureObjects`; `listObjects()` returns `fixtureObjects` plus `areaObjects`).
