### Step 1: Geometry helpers and the town.json schema

Deliver:
- Conventions (write them into the header comment of `src/world/layout/town.js`): compass +x east, +z south; an oriented rectangle is `{ x, z, w, d, rot }` with `w` along the local x axis, `d` along the local z axis and the FRONT toward `(sin rot, cos rot)` (ARCH 10.8; at `rot` 0 the front is +z, south); a rectangle with `rot` of PI/2 therefore covers `d` metres in world x and `w` metres in world z. The entrance of an anchor or plot is its front centre `(x + (d / 2) sin rot, z + (d / 2) cos rot)`, exported as `entrancePoint(rect)` from `geometry.js`. Closed rings: polygons and closed roads do NOT repeat the first point; a looping road carries `closed: true`. `facing` is a derived compass letter (`N` front toward -z, `E` +x, `S` +z, `W` -x), the one nearest to `(sin rot, cos rot)`, exported as `facingOf(rot)`; `rot` is radians and is the source of truth.
- `tests/unit/layout/geometry.test.js` first. `src/world/layout/geometry.js` (pure, no imports from three): `polygonContains(poly, x, z)`, `polygonArea(poly)` (absolute value), `polylineLength(points, closed = false)`, `pointAlongPolyline(points, d)` (clamped to the ends), `distanceToPolyline(points, x, z)`, `nearestPointOnPolyline(points, x, z)` returning `{ x, z, d, t }` (`d` distance to it, `t` metres along the line), `rectCorners(rect)`, `rectOverlap(a, b, margin = 0)` (separating-axis test; `margin` inflates both), `rectInPolygon(rect, poly)` (all four corners inside), `rectTouchesPolygon(rect, poly)` (any corner inside the polygon, any polygon vertex inside the rect, or any edges crossing), `rectDistanceToPolyline(rect, points)` (0 when the line enters the rectangle, else the smallest distance between the rectangle edges and the segments; this is the rect-vs-polyline buffer helper used by every clearance rule), `entrancePoint(rect)`, `facingOf(rot)`, `segmentIntersection(a0, a1, b0, b1)` returning the crossing point or null.
- `src/world/layout/town.js` exports `validateTown(town)` (pure, returns an array of problem strings, empty when valid) and `loadTown()` (returns the committed data through a static `import townJson from './town.json'`, which Vite and Vitest both handle; plain-Node scripts do NOT import `town.js`, they read the file with `fs`; `loadTown` also runs `validateTown` and throws on problems in dev). Schema: `{ version: 1, radius: 300, spawn: { x, z }, anchors: { <zoneId>: { x, z, rot, w, d, y? } }, areas: { sea: { level: -1.2 }, harbour: polygon, beach: polygon, forest: polygon, farmland: polygon, hill: { x, z, r, h }, square: { cx, cz, w, d } }, harbour: { quay: { points: [[x, z]...], width: 3 }, jetty: { x, z, rot, length: 55, width: 4 }, props: [] }, local: { gate?: { x, z, rot }, temple?: { x, z, rot } }, roads: [{ id, class: "main|street|lane|path", width, closed?, access?, points: [[x, z]...] }], plots: [{ id, type, x, z, w, d, rot, facing }], boats: [{ id, kind, x, z, rot, place }] }`. Meaning: `areas.sea.level` equals `WATER_LEVEL` of `src/config.js` (-1.2; the sea is everything outside the coast, no polygon); `areas.harbour` is the WATER polygon of the basin and is the only polygon that counts as water for overlap rules; `areas.hill.r` is the radius of the bump in metres (40 to 70) and `h` its peak height; `areas.square` is the centred rect (the same 56 by 44 rect as `slice.json`, so `loadSquare()` stays equal); anchor `y` is optional and is the floor height of the anchor plateau (plan 033); `harbour.quay.points` is the open polyline of the land-side quay edge (the stone strip is `width` metres wide landward of it); `harbour.jetty` x, z is the landward start centre ON the quay edge and it runs toward `(sin rot, cos rot)` for `length` metres (plans 033, 038 read exactly this); `harbour.props` is an empty array reserved for plan 038; `local.gate` and `local.temple` (optional, plan 039 fills them, rot as above); road `access` (optional) is a zone id naming the anchor this lane ends at; `boats[].kind` is `catamaran`, `boat-fishing` or `boat-rowing` and `place` is `beach`, `shallows`, `harbour` or `jetty`.
- Validator rules (each has a failing case in the test): `version` is 1 and `radius` 300; `spawn` finite numbers inside the radius; all 14 zone ids present in `anchors` (no extras) with finite `x`, `z`, `rot` and positive `w`, `d`; every anchor rectangle has all four corners inside the radius minus 5 m; `areas.sea.level` is -1.2; every polygon has at least 3 points, no repeated first point, no duplicate consecutive points, non-zero area and is inside the radius; `hill` has positive `r` and `h` between 14 and 22; `square` positive `w`, `d`; roads: unique ids, class in the enum, `width` equal to the class width (main 7, street 5, lane 3.5, path 1.8), at least 2 points, every point inside the radius minus 15 m, `closed` boolean when present (a closed road has at least 3 points), `access` a known zone id when present; `harbour.quay.points` at least 2 points, `width` 3, `jetty.length` 55 and `width` 4, the jetty end `(x + length sin rot, z + length cos rot)` inside `areas.harbour` and its root within 1 m of the quay polyline; `local` has only the keys `gate` and `temple`; the top level has NO other keys than `version radius spawn anchors areas harbour local roads plots boats`; plots: unique ids, `type` in `house-a|house-b|shop-row|cafe|market|hut`, `w` and `d` equal to the per-type size (house-a 8 by 9, house-b 9 by 10, shop-row 12 by 8, cafe 10 by 8, market 14 by 10, hut 4 by 4.5), finite `rot` and `facing` equal to `facingOf(rot)`; boats: unique ids, kind and place in the enums.
- Tests: `tests/unit/layout/geometry.test.js` covers every function above on known shapes (rotated rectangles overlapping and not, margin making a near miss overlap, a concave polygon, a rect touching a polygon only by a crossing edge, `rectDistanceToPolyline` for a line crossing, a line 5 m away and a line past a rectangle corner, distance to a segment end, `pointAlongPolyline` clamping, `polylineLength` closed vs open, `polygonArea` of a known rectangle and triangle, `entrancePoint` at rot 0 and PI/2, `facingOf` for the four compass angles and 45 degrees). `tests/unit/layout/townSchema.test.js`: a minimal valid town built in the test passes; one case per validator rule above fails with a message naming it (unknown top-level key, a repeated first point, a wrong road width, a jetty ending on land, a facing that disagrees with rot, a missing zone id, `local.gate` accepted, `local.other` rejected); `loadTown()` returns an object with `version` 1.
