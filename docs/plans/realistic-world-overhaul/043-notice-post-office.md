# Plan 043: Post Office (zone notice): resume at the counter, journey 2

Epic E4. Estimated turns: 60.

## Goal

Build the Post Office, the first converted landmark, in the style of a small town post office (cream plaster, red-oxide hip tile roof, pillared front veranda, a red pillar box, a hanging board in English and Tamil). The resume is collected at the counter: pressing `E` there downloads the PDF exactly as v1 did and shows the existing notice dialogue. Also create the shared dialogue expectation file for all 14 zones and prove journey 2. Layout in one line (building-local metres, origin at the footprint centre, +z = front/south, N = -z): a 14 by 10 footprint (x -7 to 7, z -5 to 5); the counter hall `hall` is the whole front strip (x -7 to 7, z -1 to 5), behind it at the back the `sorting-room` (x -7 to 2, z -5 to -1) and the `postmaster-office` (x 2 to 7, z -5 to -1). Every coordinate below is a target: `checkBuilding` is the judge; when the placer reports a collision or an overlap, nudge the object by at most 0.4 m inside the same zone and list it under `different_from_plan`.

This is a Rich-bar landmark: 3 to 6 rooms, at least 40 distinct object types, an enterable door, all seven exterior detail classes, and every room reachable (`npm run check:buildings -- --only notice`).
