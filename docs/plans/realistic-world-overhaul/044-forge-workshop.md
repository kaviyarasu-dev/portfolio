# Plan 044: Engineering Workshop (zone forge): tools, electronics and the skills board

Epic E4. Estimated turns: 60.

## Goal

Build the Engineering Workshop, a small-scale engineering works of the kind found in Tamil Nadu industrial lanes: a shop floor with lathes and drill presses, a welding bay, a tool store, an electronics lab and a works office; exterior with a corrugated tin roof, a half-raised roller shutter (decorative), a brick chimney, pallets, oil drums, a water tank, a lean-to and a painted signboard. The skills board on the shop floor opens the tech-skills dialogue (the dialogue content is unchanged). This plan also creates the 14 tool props (`tools` set); `cable-reel` and `compressor` are reused by plans 046, 047 and 051. Layout in one line (building-local metres, origin at the footprint centre, +z = front/south, N = -z): an 18 by 14 footprint (x -9 to 9, z -7 to 7); the rooms TILE the whole footprint, no yard and no gap: `shop-floor` (x -9 to 2, z -2 to 7, 11 by 9) and `weld-bay` (x 2 to 9, z -2 to 7, 7 by 9) form the front block, and the back strip (z -7 to -2) holds `tool-store` (x -9 to -4, 5 by 5), `electronics-lab` (x -4 to 2, 6 by 5) and `office` (x 2 to 9, 7 by 5). The tool store (5 by 5 instead of 5 by 4) and the office (7 by 5 instead of 5 by 4) are slightly larger than the first sketch and the extra fifth room `weld-bay` fills the remaining 7 by 9 m, so that the five rooms tile 18 by 14 (areas 99, 63, 25, 30, 35 = 252). Every coordinate below is a target: `checkBuilding` is the judge; when the placer reports a collision or an overlap, nudge the object by at most 0.4 m inside the same room and list it under `different_from_plan`.

This is a Rich-bar landmark: 3 to 6 rooms, at least 40 distinct object types, an enterable door, all seven exterior detail classes, and every room reachable (`npm run check:buildings -- --only forge`).
