# Plan 045: GravityWrite HQ tower (zone citadel), part 1: structure, glass facade, lobby, lift and stairs

Epic E4. Estimated turns: 60. Model: opus.

## Goal

The modern office tower of GravityWrite (zone `citadel`): five levels, a lift and a staircase, glass curtain-wall facade with steel mullions and lit floor bands, a street-level lobby with reception. This plan builds the structure, the facade and the lobby; plan 046 furnishes the upper floors, raises the manifest stage to `full` and converts the zone. Six rooms (one rectangle each): `lobby` (level 0), `newsroom` (level 1), `meeting-room` and `executive-office` (level 2), `server-room` (level 3), `rooftop` (level 4, walkable roof terrace).

Layout in one line (building-local metres, origin at the footprint centre, +z = front/south, N = -z, footprint 22 by 18: x -11 to 11, z -9 to 9; perimeter walls are 0.30 thick flush inside the footprint, so the inner faces are at x +-10.7 and z +-8.7): the stair and lift core is the north-east corner (x 4.9 to 10.65, z -8.7 to -0.5) on every level and is INSIDE one room per level. Every coordinate below is a target: `checkBuilding` is the judge; when the placer reports a collision or an overlap, nudge the object by at most 0.4 m inside the same zone and list it under `different_from_plan`. The anchor of `citadel` in `src/world/layout/town.json` is (72, -22, 22, 18) with a rotation that is a multiple of PI/2 chosen by plan 033; every world coordinate in this plan is given for rotation 0 and the recompute rule is the ARCHITECTURE 10.8 transform (world = (72 + lx cos r + lz sin r, -22 - lx sin r + lz cos r); yaw = r plus the local yaw).
