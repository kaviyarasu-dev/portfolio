# Plan 055: Lighthouse and keeper cottage (zone lighthouse): climb to the top, contact board, journey 4

Epic E4. Estimated turns: 60. Model: opus.

## Goal

Build a real lighthouse with its keeper cottage on the north-east headland. The tower is climbed by four straight flights through the three floors below the top to the lantern room, then up to a walkable gallery deck around a cone cap. The contact board in the lantern room opens the contact dialogue (LinkedIn, GitHub, email click-to-reveal; no phone number). The keeper cottage at the foot of the tower has a living-kitchen room and a bedroom. The tower looks round from outside: a cylinder skin over a square structural shell. Also prove journey 4 (walk the road from the harbour, climb, read the board).

This is a Rich-bar landmark: 3 to 6 rooms, at least 40 distinct object types, an enterable door, all seven exterior detail classes, and every room reachable (`npm run check:buildings -- --only lighthouse`).
