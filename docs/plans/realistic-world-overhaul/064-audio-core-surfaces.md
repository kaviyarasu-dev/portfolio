# Plan 064: Audio core: sound bank, event sounds and footsteps by surface

Epic E6. Estimated turns: 55.

## Goal

Rebuild the audio layer around three ideas: a sound bank (every sound id resolves to files or to a generated fallback), a footstep system that picks the sound from the surface under the player (grass, sand, gravel, wood, tile, carpet, stone, metal), and event sounds for doors, switches, sitting, lifts and interaction. All new sounds are generated by `scripts/assets/synthSfx.mjs` (no other audio host is reachable, ARCHITECTURE.md 10.1), CC0 in the ledger. A test fails if any walkable surface of the world is not mapped to a sound.

### Step 3: Footsteps and event sounds, Playwright

Deliver:
- `src/systems/audio/footsteps.js`: `FootstepSystem.update(dtMs, player)`: when the player is grounded and moving, accumulate distance; a step every 0.62 m of walk stride (so cadence about 0.5 s walking, 0.32 s running); the surface comes from `currentSurface(player)`: inside a building room (`instance.insideRoom` with the room `surface` or `floorMaterial`), on a stair of a room with surface metal use `metal`, on the jetty `wood`, wading `sand` with the `step_water` splash, else `surfaceAt(x, z)` from plan 034; volume 0.25 walking and 0.4 running with a random 0.9 to 1.1 rate; variants rotate without repeating the same twice in a row. Footsteps stop when sitting, in first-person lift rides and while airborne.
- Event sounds: subscribe to the event bus (use the exact event names that plans 017 and 018 emit, for example `door:open`, `door:close`, `door:locked`, `sfx:switch`, `sfx:sit`, `sfx:stand`, `sfx:lift-ding`, `sfx:lift-move`: list them from the code and map each to a sound id: door open and close with a position, `door:locked`, `sfx:switch`, `sfx:sit`, `sfx:stand`, `sfx:lift-ding`, `sfx:lift-move` loop start and stop, interact stinger, typing tick, resume pick-up). Document the mapping in `docs/world-conventions.md`.
- Hook `audioState()` returns `{ unlocked, muted, master, lastFootstep: { surface, file, t }, lastEvents: [...last 10 ids] }`. `tests/e2e/footsteps.spec.js`: press a key to unlock; `teleport` to a grass spot, walk with `w` for 1.5 s: `lastFootstep.surface` is `grass`; the beach sand (a known beach point) gives `sand`; the main street `stone`; the hotel lobby `stone` (marble) and a guest room `wood` or `carpet` per its manifest; the lighthouse stairs `metal` if the room surface says so; the quay `gravel` or `stone` per the surface lookup; opening a door records `door_open` in `lastEvents`; zero console errors.
